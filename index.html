Research
⭐ Job Market Paper
Pricing Regulation and Health Insurance Coverage: Evidence from the ACA's Gender Rating Ban
Why it matters: Before the ACA, insurers could charge women up to 50% more than men for the same health plan. This paper asks: when that ended, did women actually gain coverage — and where did it matter most?
Estimates the effect of the Affordable Care Act's prohibition of gender-based pricing on nongroup health insurance coverage using American Community Survey microdata and a difference-in-differences design. Results show increased coverage among women in states with higher pre-policy exposure to gender rating, providing evidence on how anti-discrimination policy affects insurance access.
Presented at APPAM Fall Research Conference, Boston, MA · 2026

Generative AI and Firm Hiring Demand: Evidence from Advanced and Developing Economies

Studies how ChatGPT's release affected firm hiring demand using 115.9 million job postings across 200 economies. Firms primarily adjust along the composition margin — shifting away from AI-substitutable roles — with heterogeneous dynamics across advanced and developing economies.

Generative AI Exposure and Healthcare Hiring: Timing Evidence from Employer Job Postings

Uses a firm-state-occupation-quarter panel of U.S. healthcare job postings 2018–2026 to estimate how generative AI affected healthcare hiring, separating post-ChatGPT changes from pandemic dynamics and the 2022–2023 white-collar slowdown.
